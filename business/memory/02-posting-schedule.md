## A+B Content Strategy Mix

### A — Builder/Share (80%)
Teach marketing, share frameworks, break down strategies. Pure value. No ask. This builds the audience.

### B — Audit Offer (20%)
Natural escalation from teaching. "I teach this, and I also do it for clients." Free audit CTA. Shows what you do without telling.

## Lead Magnet Rule

**Every post with lead potential gets a matching lead magnet.**

| Post Type | Lead Magnet | CTA |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| Copywriting lesson | "Steal my 10 highest-converting headlines" | "Comment HEADLINES and I'll send it" |
| Funnel breakdown | "My exact 5-step funnel checklist" | "Comment FUNNEL and I'll send it" |
| Email sequence | "7 email templates that print money" | "Comment EMAIL and I'll send it" |
| Positioning teardown | "Positioning audit template" | "Comment AUDIT and I'll send it" |
| Newsletter growth | "Newsletter growth checklist" | "Comment NEWSLETTER and I'll send it" |

**Workflow:**
1. Write post → Identify lead potential
2. Create matching PDF/checklist/template
3. Upload to Google Drive via Zapier
4. Include CTA in post
5. Track who comments → DM with link → Capture email

## Daily Content Production

### Phase 1: Morning (9 AM)
- Check analytics from yesterday
- Review what performed
- Determine today's focus

### Phase 2: Planning (9:30 AM)
- Pick topic based on weekly rhythm
- Assign mission tag
- Create lead magnet if needed

### Phase 3: Writing (10 AM - 12 PM)
- Content Planner → brief for all platforms
- Content Writer → execute brief
- Humanizer → strip AI patterns
- OPEX review → quality gates

### Phase 4: Design (1 PM - 3 PM)
- Akaka designs all visuals
- OPEX provides direction
- Never execute design

### Phase 5: Publishing (4 PM)
- Push to Notion Content Calendar (PRODUCTION mode)
- Akaka posts manually
- Schedule for optimal times

### Phase 6: Engagement (5 PM - 6 PM)
- Respond to comments
- Engage with similar content
- Track DMs and leads
