# Milestones & Activation Logic

**Last updated:** 2026-07-01

## Core Milestones (6-Month Roadmap)
| # | Milestone | Target Date | Success Metric |
|---|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | Launch Day-1 Content Pack (Medium article + LinkedIn + IG carousel + Stories) | Day 1 | Publish on all active platforms |
| 2 | Reach $100 USD in Medium Partner earnings | End of Week 1 | $100 earned |
| 3 | First Paying Client | End of Week 2 | Signed contract >= $500 |
| 4 | 5K followers across platforms | End of Month 1 | Combined follower count >= 5,000 |
| 5 | 10K email subscribers | End of Month 2 | List size >= 10,000 |
| 6 | $10K cumulative revenue | End of Month 3 | Revenue >= $10,000 |
| 7 | $30K cumulative revenue | End of Month 4 | Revenue >= $30,000 |
| 8 | 30K followers + 30K subscribers | End of Month 5 | Combined audience >= 30,000 |
| 9 | $100K total revenue (goal) | End of Month 6 | Revenue >= $100,000 |

## Activation Logic (When to Trigger Agents)
- **Milestone 1** -> Activate Content Planner to generate the Day-1 content. Provide visual direction for Akaka to design in Open Design.
- **Milestone 2** -> Trigger Research Agent to track Medium earnings and adjust content frequency.
- **Milestone 3** -> Launch Sales Agent to build quick-turn proposal for client.
- **Milestone 4-5** -> Run Growth Loop (social posting + repurposing) via Content Planner daily.
- **Milestone 6-9** -> Initiate Revenue Ops workflow: pricing review, upsell funnel, offer design.

## How to Mark a Milestone Complete
1. Update the corresponding entry in 03-goals-and-missions.md under Milestones Completed.
2. Post a short status update in chat (no emojis) confirming the achievement.
3. The next milestone's agents are automatically queued.

## Example Milestone Completion Message
"Milestone 3 completed: First paying client signed for $1,200. Next step -- launch the onboarding flow and start the referral program."
