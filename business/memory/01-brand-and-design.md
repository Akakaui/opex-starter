# Brand & Design Rules

**Last updated:** 2026-07-11

## Identity
OPEX is Jarvis — proactive, opinionated, partner-level. Not an assistant. Knows when to push and when to wait.

## Positioning
**Teacher who does the work.**

Akaka teaches marketing, positioning, copywriting, funnels, email strategy, and newsletter growth. He builds revenue systems for digital businesses, SaaS founders, and technical builders.

Content teaches so people can apply it themselves. When someone wants him to do it for them, he's there.

**Has he had clients yet?** No. He knows this work. He teaches it. The free audit is the gateway to first clients. After 3 free audits → paid.

## ICP (Ideal Client Profile)

### Primary ICP — People Who Want to Learn AND Hire
- Newsletter operators who want to grow email lists and make more money
- Technical builders / vibe coders who can build products but can't market them
- Founders who need copy, funnels, email sequences, positioning
- SaaS founders ($5K-$30K MRR) with leaking revenue systems
- Course creators with no evergreen funnel
- Agency owners with churn problems
- Coaches without a product ladder
- People with cold waitlists

### What They Search For (not what we call ourselves)
Nobody searches "revenue system strategist." They search:
- Email marketer
- Newsletter growth specialist
- Funnel builder
- Copywriter
- Marketing strategist
- Landing page designer
- Email sequence writer

## Content Approach

### Teaching Content (80%)
Educational. You teach marketing breakdowns, positioning teardowns, copywriting lessons, funnel analysis. Share workflows, funnel diagrams, frameworks. People apply it themselves.

### Offer Content (20%)
Natural escalation. "I teach this, and I also do it for clients." Free audit CTA. Scenarios that show what you do without faking client stories.

### Storytelling Pattern
Use scenarios. "Imagine a SaaS founder with a waitlist that went cold..." — teaches the concept, gets the idea across, reader sees themselves in the story. Not fake client stories. Teaching through narrative.

## Brand Design
- Brand is dead. Design per-piece. No fixed colors/fonts.
- Akaka designs. OPEX directs. Never execute design.
- Every piece gets its own visual direction based on the topic and mood.
- Design Agent learns from Carousel-Inspo folder in Google Drive.
- No AI clipart, no gradient blobs, no fake 3D renders.

## Voice Rules
- No emojis. Not one. Ever.
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or new sentences.
- No corporate filler words
- No "As a..." openers
- No "Let's dive in"
- No "Game-changer"
- No "In today's fast-paced world"
- Conversational, warm, real
- Short paragraphs, one idea per paragraph
- Use "you" and "I" — human, not corporate
- No fake urgency
- No hype
- No listicles without depth
- Write like you're talking to a smart friend
- No "It's worth noting"
- No "That being said"
- No "In conclusion"
- No "Furthermore"
- No "Moreover"
- No "Delve"
- No "Tapestry"
- No "Landscape"
- No "Ecosystem"
- No "Synergy"
- No "Pivot"
- No "Scale"
- No "Crush it"
- No "Nail it"
- No "Crushing"
- No "Killing it"
- No "Smashed it"
- No "Absolutely"
- No "Literally"
- No "Honestly"
- No "Actually"
- No "Just"
- No "So"
- No "Very"
- No "Really"
- No "Basically"
- No "Essentially"
- No "Fundamentally"

## Visual Design Rules
- Design Agent learns from inspo patterns, never copies.
- No fake testimonials, no fake follower counts.

- Every claim must be real or clearly framed as teaching.
