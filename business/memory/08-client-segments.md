# Client Segments Overview

## Overview
Based on your positioning "I build the marketing layer for technical builders who can build but can't market," here are the three client segments you should target and serve:

## 1. Revenue Strategy Client

### Who They Are
SaaS founders at $5K-$30K MRR

### Profile
- Can build technically but are stuck scaling past first wave of customers
- Need someone to fix revenue leaks, build funnels, and provide battle-tested tactics
- Both revenue and productivity come together here

### Emotional Levers
- Frustrated by $5K → $15K ceilings
- Fear of hitting a ceiling and burning out before funding
- Anxiety about revenue not scaling

### Where To Find Them
- Facebook groups: "SaaS growth," "revenue systems," "launch strategy"
- Slack: "Revenue Hackers"
- LinkedIn: Search fund raises + comments on conversion rate optimization

### What They Care About
- Revenue leaks
- Cold waitlists
- Converting leads to paying clients
- Email marketing and funnel expertise
- Dramatic conversion rate improvements

### Offer That Clicks
Revenue system audit ($500-$5000) + 30-day performance roadmap

### Authority Hook
"I've fixed SaaS revenue leaks for companies going from $5K to $30K MRR. You can fix yours for less time."

## 2. Business Productivity Client

### Who They Are
Course creators, coaches, small agencies with revenue but fighting systems

### Profile
- Have products (course, coaching, service)
- Build the product side, but everything else is messy
- Seeking systems that enable 10x what they do today

### Emotional Levers
- Energy drain by repetitive admin
- Fear of hiring the wrong person
- Feeling behind on operations as they grow

### Where To Find Them
- Facebook: "productivity hacks," "systems automation," "business scaling"
- Indie hacker forums
- Reddit r/productivity
- Slack: creator communities

### What They Care About
- Building repeatable systems
- Automation without tech skills
- Workflows that let them run business without being everywhere

### Offer That Clicks
Business productivity system ($500-$5000) + workflow automation guide

### Authority Hook
"I help build systems that make businesses run themselves. You focus on the product, I'll build the business."

## 3. Vibe Coder Builder Client

### Who They Are
Technical builders who can build but can't market

### Profile
- Build tools, apps, SaaS projects themselves
- Can code and love building things
- Don't know how to market or get paying customers

### Emotional Levers
- Frustrated by zero traction
- No money despite awesome tool
- Everything wants to try product but nobody pays

### Where To Find Them
- Facebook: Vibe coder, no-code, SaaS builders communities
- Twitter: Indie hackers
- Slack: R&D communities

### What They Care About
- Building a base
- Getting first paying customers
- Understanding why people would pay for their tool
- Marketing that resonates with builders

### Offer That Clicks
"Builder marketing system" ($500-$5000) + marketing automation tools

### Authority Hook
"I was a vibe coder too. Built tools, sold them. I know what works."

1. Join 5 Facebook groups about SaaS growth, revenue systems, launch strategy
2. Comment on posts EVERY DAY for 2 weeks
3. Pull 3-5 prospects to DMs
4. Value: "Your SaaS is at $5K → $15K ceiling. Here are the 3 spots leaking $10K daily."

### Productivity Client

2. Comment heavily for 2 weeks
3. Pull 3-5 prospects to DMs
