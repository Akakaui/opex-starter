## Content Strategy

### A — Builder/Share (80%)
Teach marketing, share frameworks, break down strategies. Pure value. No ask. This builds the audience.

### B — Audit Offer (20%)
Natural escalation from teaching. "I teach this, and I also do it for clients." Free audit CTA. Shows what you do without telling.

### Content Pillars

1. **Marketing Fundamentals** — Positioning, messaging, value props, ICP definition
2. **Copywriting** — Headlines, hooks, email sequences, landing pages, sales copy
3. **Funnels** — Lead capture, nurture sequences, conversion optimization, checkout flows
4. **Email Marketing** — Newsletter growth, automation, segmentation, deliverability
5. **Audience Growth** — Content strategy, social media, community building, partnerships
6. **Revenue Systems** — Pricing, monetization, product ladders, recurring revenue
7. **Technical Marketing** — Tools, automation, analytics, tracking, integration
